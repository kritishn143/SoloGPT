## 🚀 What is SoloGPT?

SoloGPT lets you run powerful large-language-model (LLM) AI locally on your mobile device. All inference happens on-device — your conversations never leave your phone.

- **Fast** – no round-trips to a remote server, responses start immediately.
- **Private** – your data stays on your device, always.
- **Offline** – works without a Wi-Fi or mobile-data connection.

## 🛠️ Getting Started

1. **Install** the app from the [Google Play Store](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=app.sologpt.ai).
2. **Download a model** – on first launch, select and download an AI model from the in-app library.
3. **Start chatting** – once the model is loaded, open a new chat and start talking to your private AI companion.

> **Note:** The initial model download requires an internet connection. After that, the app works completely offline.

---

## 🔐 Privacy

SoloGPT is built with privacy as a first principle:

- No account or sign-in required.
- No telemetry or analytics collected.
- All data (chat history, model files) resides locally on your device.
- The app requests only the minimum permissions necessary to function.
